# 📊 E-commerce SQL Analysis – Business Insights

## 🟦 1. Sales Performance

- The Electronics category generates the highest revenue, indicating strong demand for electronic products.
- Overall sales performance shows that a few key categories contribute significantly to total revenue.

---

## 🟨 2. Product Performance

- Adidas T-shirt, Nike Shoes, and Bed are the top-selling products based on quantity sold.
- Clothing and essential household items dominate sales, suggesting consistent customer demand for these categories.

---

## 🟩 3. Customer Behavior

- Customer spending varies significantly, with a small group of customers contributing higher revenue.
- High-value customers (above average spending) play a key role in overall business revenue.

---

## 🟪 4. Regional (City-wise) Analysis

- Chennai generates the highest revenue, followed by Hyderabad and Bangalore.
- Revenue is concentrated in metro cities, while smaller cities like Ahmedabad contribute less to overall sales.

---

## 🟧 5. Sales Trends (Time-based)

- Monthly sales trends show variation, indicating possible seasonal demand patterns.
- Certain months experience higher revenue, suggesting peak sales periods.

---

## 🟥 6. Payment Behavior

- UPI is the most preferred payment method among customers.
- Digital payment methods dominate transactions, indicating a shift towards cashless payments.

---

## 🚀 Conclusion

This analysis highlights key business drivers such as top-performing products, high-revenue cities, and valuable customers. The insights can help businesses focus on high-demand categories, target profitable customers, and optimize sales strategies for better decision-making.
